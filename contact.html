ORIGIN & MISSION

Born on the Electronics Repair Bench

VOLTARIS was founded in Austin, Texas by hardware engineers and logistics veterans frustrated by enterprise ERP software that couldn't tell an iPhone motherboard from an open-ear sports headset.

Our mission is simple: provide consumer electronics studios, warranty service centers, and gadget distributors with sub-millisecond precision, uncompromising local performance, and zero-bullshit operational clarity.

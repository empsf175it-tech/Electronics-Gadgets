Electronics Operations Glossary

Key standards, identifiers, and telemetry metrics utilized across the VOLTARIS platform.

A globally unique 15-digit number allocated to cellular devices. The first 8 digits (TAC - Type Allocation Code) designate the exact hardware model and manufacturer, followed by the serial sequence and Luhn algorithm check digit.

The formal transactional approval required to dispatch defective, dead-on-arrival (DOA), or repair-bound electronics back to wholesale suppliers and OEM manufacturers for credit or replacement.

The percentage of received inventory sold to end-users within a predetermined interval (typically 30 or 60 days). Essential for anticipating obsolete gadget depreciation before successor models enter the market.

Interoperable smart home networking standards connecting sensors, lighting, and hubs across Apple Home, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa ecosystems without proprietary gateway lock-in.

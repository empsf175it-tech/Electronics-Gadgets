Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE: OCTOBER 2026 // REVISION 3.4

1. Zero-CDN Operational Model

VOLTARIS is engineered to operate without external content delivery networks (CDNs). All script assets, fonts, telemetry calculation engines, and styling tokens are hosted directly from your licensed cluster or self-hosted container instance. Your device serials and financial metrics are never routed through third-party advertising or analytics networks.

2. Telemetry & Serial Number Data Handling

We process 15-digit IMEI sequences, serial numbers, MAC addresses, and barcode scans strictly for hardware inventory reconciliation, warranty claim tracking, and replenishment forecasting. We do not sell, broker, or aggregate serialized device histories across independent tenant studios.

3. Cryptographic Storage & Access Governance

All database records are encrypted at rest using AES-256. Transport layer communications utilize TLS 1.3 with mandatory forward secrecy. Role-based access control (RBAC) ensures only authorized roles within your organization can decrypt and export financial cost figures.

4. Inquiries & Data Protection Officer

Direct inquiries regarding SOC 2 audit packages or GDPR device deletion requests to: privacy@voltaris.demo.
